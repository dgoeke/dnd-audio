## Accepted P1 findings

### 1. Candidate coverage was not Cartesian acoustic evidence

`_weakest_correlation` required every candidate on each side to appear in some measured pair,
but diagonal `A1↔B1` and `A2↔B2` evidence satisfied that test while leaving `A1↔B2` and
`A2↔B1` unmeasured. The contained-fragment path could therefore delete the shorter text
without the every-pair evidence ADR-0033 promised.

**Disposition:** require the measured pair keys to equal the full Cartesian product of both
segments' candidate ids. A two-candidate-by-two-candidate negative regression proves diagonal
coverage keeps both segments; its full-Cartesian control collapses the fragment.

### 2. Retry stitching projected child answers through the parent request

A resolved truncation kept the original parent `RequestPlan` and only child request-id strings.
Assembly consequently recorded the parent's padded bounds and exposed every stitched word to
every parent ownership piece. With independently configurable grace and padding, a word returned
by one child could be claimed by a different child's candidate, and the records could not show
which submitted occurrence produced it.

**Disposition:** `RequestOutcome` now retains the actual leaf submission plans and their word
occurrences. Assembly builds ownership separately for each leaf, records that child's sliced
activity ownership and padded bounds, and compares its words only with its pieces. The discarded
truncated parent remains in attempted request lineage but is not an ownership occurrence.
Regressions assert exact child bounds, boundary-repeat lineage, atomic fallback to the parent,
and the counterexample where a left-child padding word must not be donated to a right-child
candidate.

## Accepted P2 finding

### 3. Legacy first-pass compatibility lacked independent complete output

The suite tested individual legacy decisions and the new pass ordering, but no independent
artifact froze every old verdict field, rejected-alternative field and decision detail. An
unasserted representation regression could pass while the charter claimed compatibility.

**Disposition:** `tests/data/transcript-collapse-main-9421d03.json` was produced by executing
the pre-M9 `main` implementation at `9421d03`, not the branch under test. It freezes four
representative shapes: ordinary correlated collapse, unrelated overlap, weak correlation with
compelling source dominance, and exact short matching speech. The branch must match the complete
legacy projection byte-for-value, and every case must omit the M9-only collapse rule.
